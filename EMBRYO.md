# Embryo Cache

Embryo Cache is Embryo-Zero's fork of [valkey](https://github.com/valkey-io/valkey) (40e00fc, commit 40e00fc). It does in-memory key-value store (Redis-compatible).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
